# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing: React + Vite, Tailwind CSS 3, Framer Motion, Lucide React. Static site, GitHub Pages deploy (`gh-pages`), base path `/natuSoul/`, .com.br domain via Registro.br.

## Users
Mobile-first visitors arriving from Instagram, Google Maps or word of mouth. Four groups, all confirmed: locals looking to relax (massage, day spa), brides and gift buyers (Day Spa Noiva, combos), tourists and travelers looking for an experience in nature, and people seeking aesthetics and health care (laser hair removal, facial/body aesthetics, nutrition consultation).

## Product Purpose
Natu Soul Estética & SPA is a day spa and aesthetics clinic. The site exists to turn visitors into WhatsApp conversations with a pre-filled message naming the service they chose. Success is a WhatsApp contact, not a page view.

## Positioning
A spa inside nature (log-cabin building, garden, water mirror, stone wall with the NS monogram) that combines spa, aesthetics and health services under one roof, including Head Spa, photo shoots and bridal day spa. Confirmed differentiators: nature setting, uncommon services, complete aesthetics and spa offer.

## Operating Context
Local business. Contact happens on WhatsApp. Address links to Google Maps. The owner supplies content (text, photos, prices) in stages.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Ten services: Massagens, Day Spa, Estética Corporal, Ensaio Fotográfico, Combos, Day Spa Noiva, Estética Facial, Depilação a Laser, Consulta Nutricional, Head Spa. Details and prices per service are pending from the owner and will be shown once supplied. Language: Brazilian Portuguese. Light/dark theme (dark by time of day, manual toggle). Undecided: real WhatsApp number, address, opening hours, Instagram handle (the owner confirmed these can be shown but has not yet provided them).

## Brand Commitments
Name: Natu Soul Estética & SPA. Logo: NS monogram with a leaf, dark green wordmark in a serif face, "Estética & SPA" in brown (`src/assets/logo.png`, transparent PNG). Welcome text written by the owner is binding: "Sejam bem vindos ao Natu Soul SPA! Um lugar aconchegante, com belas paisagens e próximo à natureza com toda a tranquilidade que você busca encontrar. Aqui, você terá um dia relaxante para seu corpo e mente para fugir da correria do dia a dia." Existing brand material (reference screenshot) uses deep green leaf photography and a translucent text card.

## Evidence on Hand
Four real photos in `src/assets/foto1-4.webp` (water mirror entrance, wooden walkway among leaves, facade at night, wooden reception with garden view) and the logo. No testimonials exist: do not invent any. No prices, address, hours or Instagram handle yet.

## Product Principles
1. One action: every section leads to a WhatsApp message.
2. Real photos of the real place beat stock imagery or decoration.
3. Never invent proof, prices or claims; show a section only when its content exists.
4. Mobile first, fast on a mid-range phone.
5. Calm is the product: the experience of the page should lower the visitor's pulse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Maintain contrast in both themes.
